# Insurance incidents involving a third party

1. Open Insurance Claims and choose Report incident.
2. Enter your fleet vehicle and the incident details as usual.
3. Under Third-party involvement, select Another person, vehicle or property was involved. This is already selected and required when the incident type is Third party.
4. Choose the affected party type, such as Two wheeler, Car, Pedestrian or Property.
5. Enter the person or owner's name, contact number, and a description of their damage or injuries. For a vehicle, also enter its registration number. Enter None in the damage/injuries field if there were none.
6. Add their address, insurer and policy number if available; these are optional.
You may optionally attach photos and videos under each third party. These files are shown under that party in the saved claim. All incident and third-party files together must be no more than ten files and 100 MB. Third-party uploads do not replace the required main incident photo and video.

7. If more people or vehicles were involved, choose Add another third party and complete each entry. You can record up to ten parties.
8. Add police information if available. Attach at least one photo and one video, then submit the report.
9. Open the saved claim and check Third-party details. These details are also available to authorized claim reviewers.

If no other party was involved, leave the involvement checkbox unselected. Existing incident details and evidence requirements still apply.
